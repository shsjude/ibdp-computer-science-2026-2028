IB Diploma Computer Science

A two-year journey through programming, computer systems, data, networks, databases, machine learning and software creation.

Explore Year 1 Explore Year 2

Course overview

What to expect

The course moves from guided programming and technical investigations toward independent software development, the Internal Assessment and final examination preparation. Theme A and Theme B are developed alongside one another so that concepts are applied through practical work.

1

Learn

Build accurate understanding of computing concepts and terminology.

2

Apply

Use Python, data and algorithms to investigate authentic problems.

3

Create

Design, program, test and document increasingly complex solutions.

4

Demonstrate

Complete practical assessments, exam questions and IA milestones.

Two-year programme map

Scope and sequence

Dates are intended as a clear guide for students and may be adjusted slightly to support learning progress, school events and assessment needs.

Year 1

August 2026–June 2027

  1. August–September 2026
    01

    Python, Data and Intelligent Systems

    Python foundations, problem-solving, lists, functions, files, NumPy, pandas, data exploration and an introduction to machine learning.

  2. October–November 2026
    02

    Computer Systems and Hardware

    CPU, GPU, registers, buses, memory, storage, the fetch-decode-execute cycle, compression and cloud services.

  3. November–December 2026
    03

    Computational Thinking and Algorithms

    Problem specifications, decomposition, abstraction, flowcharts, debugging, exception handling, searching, sorting and efficiency.

  4. January–February 2027
    04

    Object-Oriented Programming

    Classes, objects, methods, constructors, UML, encapsulation and the relevant HL extensions.

  5. March–April 2027
    05

    Databases and Data Applications

    Relational design, ERDs, normalization, SQL queries and the development of data-driven applications.

  6. May–June 2027
    06

    Software Creation and IA Foundations

    User needs, success criteria, prototypes, testing, documentation and the early planning stages of the Internal Assessment.

Year 2

August 2027–May 2028

  1. August–September 2027
    07

    Operating Systems, Logic and Architecture

    Binary and hexadecimal, logic gates, operating systems, scheduling, interrupts, control systems and translation.

  2. September–October 2027
    08

    Machine Learning and Responsible AI

    Data preparation, regression, classification, clustering, model evaluation, neural networks and ethical implications.

  3. October–December 2027
    09

    Networks, Cloud and Cybersecurity

    Network devices, protocols, addressing, packet switching, routing, encryption, certificates, vulnerabilities and security.

  4. August–December 2027
    IA

    Internal Assessment Development

    Design, implementation, testing, documentation and evaluation of an independently developed programming solution.

  5. January–February 2028
    10

    Advanced Structures and Problem-Solving

    HL abstract data types, integrated programming problems, algorithmic thinking and Paper 2 preparation.

  6. February–March 2028
    11

    Case Study and Mock Examinations

    Case-study research, unfamiliar scenarios, timed practice, mock examinations and targeted feedback.

  7. April–May 2028
    12

    Final Revision and Examinations

    Consolidation of course content, correction of misconceptions, past-paper practice and final examination preparation.

The Internal Assessment is developed alongside the Year 2 units rather than being treated as a separate end-of-course activity.

Assessment pathway
